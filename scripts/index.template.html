04 — FAQ

You're probably
wondering…

Do we need technical staff to work with you?

No. We handle everything — discovery, build, integration and maintenance. If your team can use email, they can use what we build.

What tools do you integrate with?

Whatever you already run: Gmail or Outlook, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Xero, QuickBooks, Notion, Airtable, Shopify and 200+ others. No rip-and-replace.

How much does it cost?

The audit is free. Builds are fixed-price from $4k depending on scope, and ongoing care plans start at $900/month. Every proposal shows projected hours saved next to the price.

Is our data safe?

Yes. Least-privilege access, encrypted credentials, EU/US data residency options, and we never train models on your data. SOC 2-aligned processes throughout.

What if an automation breaks?

Every automation ships with monitoring and alerting. Most issues are fixed before you notice; care-plan clients get a 4-hour response SLA.

Make.com, n8n or Zapier — which one should we choose?

It depends on what you automate: Make.com is the price-performance champion for most SMBs, n8n is the flexible, self-hostable option, and Zapier has the widest integration catalog. In the free audit we'll tell you which fits your processes — we build on all three.
